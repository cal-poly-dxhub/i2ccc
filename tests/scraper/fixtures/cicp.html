Farther, Faster, Higher: CICP Helps You and Your Institution Become Cloud Superheroes

The Internet2 Cloud Infrastructure Community Program (CICP) is more than just a forum for higher education and research organizations to work together to maximize the value of their investments in public cloud platforms. It is a framework where your team and your entire institution can share, grow, learn, and master public cloud technologies to serve the research and academic mission of your organization.

As part of CICP you will:

Accelerate cloud adoption
Master cloud technology
Influence cloud providers
Expand your knowledge network

CICP is your Cloudforce Multiplier.

CICP is a Cloudforce Multiplier

What does “Cloudforce Multiplier” mean?

The CICP is a community of peers which meets regularly to expand its members’ cloud expertise, capabilities, and capacities.  The CICP benefits IT leaders and their teams through:

  • Developing new skills: The CICP community, Internet2 facilitators, and experts from cloud service providers are available to answer questions – big and small – about what your institution wants to do in the cloud.
  • Accelerating cloud adoption: Leaders and their teams can offer more to their institution, more quickly thanks to knowledge from the experiences of community peers.
  • Sharing technical substance: Deep dives into AWS and GCP cloud services, problem solving, and sharing lessons learned within the community accelerates adoption, increases security, and can save money.
  • Amplify your voice: Collective and direct input to AWS and GCP can affect changes with cloud vendors.
  • Stronger cloud partner relationships: CICP engagement can drive stronger, more direct and more productive relationships between institutions and their cloud vendors

Community activities like peer discussions, working groups, expert presentations, joint training on timely topics are foundational to the NET+ Amazon Web Services (AWS) and NET+ Google Cloud Platform (GCP) programs and core to the CICP.

Our meetings include:

  • Regular and curated strategy discussions
  • Bi-weekly “open-mic” technology calls
  • Topical webinars
  • Deep-dives on challenges identified by members

CICP members work together to focus our voice with cloud service providers. Together, small teams and individual institutions now have an opportunity for stronger, more direct, and more productive relationships with cloud vendors.

CICP subscribers can now receive an annual voucher to use toward CLASS training. Voucher values vary by subscription tier and help reduce participation costs. To check your institution’s voucher balance or redeem it, please contact class@internet2.edu.

CICP Benefits & Fees

Benefits Include: 

  • Apply CICP training vouchers and discounts to participate in peer training cohorts for cloud certifications, or other skills through Internet2’s Internet2 CLASS program.
  • Gain insights into your institution’s cloud deployment by participating in Internet2’s Data Benchmarking Service. Compare usage data with peers, find data-driven ways to refine cloud efficiency, and identify leaders in specific cloud services to benefit from their expertise.
  • Leverage Internet2 technical consulting. CICP members can receive advice, a second opinion, pointers to resources, introductions to knowledgeable members, and other assistance to ideate on a solution, better understand an approach, or streamline an implementation from Internet2 staff.
  • Learn with and from peers and cloud experts through a variety of regularly scheduled calls and activities on topics driven by community interest.
  • Confer with AWS and GCP technical experts who actively participate in CICP calls and who are available to share insights, and call upon experts from other parts of their organizations to benefit CICP members.
  • Invite your community to participate to maximize your institution’s CICP membership – IT professionals, engineers, researchers, IT leadership, educators and students.

CICP Dependent Services:

While any eligible organization may join the Cloud Infrastructure Community Program (CICP), institutional subscription to CICP is a requirement for all organizations participating in:

  • NET+ AWS Service

Annual Fees:

  • There is a one-time $1,000 registration fee for joining the program. If your organization signs the agreement without additional modifications, we will waive this registration fee in recognition of lowering our administrative and legal costs.
  • Internet2 may consider a grant award for organizations based on need.
  • The Cloud Infrastructure Community Program was previously known as Infrastructure and Platform Services (IPS) program, which featured a Participation Agreement (PA) and Participation Schedule (PS). Those still using the IPS are full subscribers to CICP as long as they have a valid PA and PS in place.

*Multi-Campus Institutions: If your organization comprises institutions with separate Carnegie Classifications, your Annual CICP Fees will be determined by the institution with the highest classification.
